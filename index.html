clipd

A self-hosted tool that turns Twitch clips into vertical videos and publishes them to your TikTok account.

What it does

  1. Finds popular clips on the Twitch channels and categories you follow (public Twitch API).
  2. Edits each clip into a vertical 9:16 video with captions and a caption text suggestion, on your own device.
  3. You review every video in the clipd dashboard: approve, retouch or reject it.
  4. For an approved video, you open “Publish to TikTok”, check the caption, choose who can see it, which interactions are allowed (comments, duet, stitch) and whether it is commercial content, then agree to the post. clipd publishes it to your TikTok account (Content Posting API) as soon as possible or at the time you chose, and shows its status.

Nothing is sent to TikTok until you agree to a post. clipd runs on your own computer or Raspberry Pi; there is no clipd server and no clipd account.

Content rights

Twitch clips belong to their streamers. clipd credits the streamer on each video, and you should only publish clips of streamers who allowed you to do so.
