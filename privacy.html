Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 22, 2026

1. Who we are

clipd is a self-hosted tool published by yassin, contact yassinam.pro@gmail.com. clipd runs on the user's own device (a computer or a Raspberry Pi). There is no clipd server: the data below is stored on that device only and is never sent to us.

2. Data clipd uses

  • TikTok account, when you connect it: your TikTok user identifier (open_id), display name and profile picture (scope user.info.basic), the access and refresh tokens TikTok issues, and the posting options TikTok returns for your account (privacy levels, interaction settings, maximum video length).
  • Your posts: the videos you approve, their caption and the settings you choose (privacy, comments, duet, stitch, commercial content disclosure), sent to TikTok to publish them (scopes video.upload, video.publish), and the publishing status TikTok returns.
  • Twitch: public clip data (titles, channels, view counts, video files) from the Twitch API, used to make the videos.
  • Dashboard: a password and session cookie to protect the dashboard on your local network.

3. Why

Only to show you which TikTok account a video will be published to, to publish the videos you agree to, and to show their status. clipd has no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and does not sell or share data with anyone other than TikTok and Twitch for the requests above.

4. Storage and security

The data is kept in a local database on your device. Tokens are never logged or displayed. Connections to TikTok and Twitch use HTTPS. The page at this address only forwards TikTok's login response to your device and stores nothing.

5. Retention and deletion

TikTok tokens and account details are kept until you disconnect the account in the clipd dashboard, which deletes them from your device and revokes clipd's access at TikTok. You can also remove clipd's access from your TikTok settings. Video files are deleted automatically a few days after publication. Uninstalling clipd removes everything.

6. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access, correct and delete your data, to restrict or object to its processing, and to lodge a complaint with the CNIL (cnil.fr). Since your data stays on your own device, you can exercise these rights directly; for any question, write to yassinam.pro@gmail.com.

7. Children

clipd is not intended for children under 16.
